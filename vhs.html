About Us

Shaping brilliant futures through quality education.

Vikas High School is a reputed higher secondary institution nestled in the heart of Agrasain Colony, Sirsa. As a private, unaided, and co-educational school, we focus on providing an inclusive and supportive environment where every child feels valued.

  • Strict adherence to the HBSE board guidelines, preparing students for board exams with confidence.
  • A modern, English-medium approach that bridges local values with global opportunities.
  • Mentored by a dedicated team of highly qualified teachers who are passionate about student success.
