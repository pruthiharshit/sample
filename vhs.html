About Our School

Nurturing Minds in the Heart of Sirsa

Located in Agrasain Colony, Vikas High School has been a cornerstone of quality education in Sirsa since its establishment in 1992. As a private, unaided institution, we are committed to maintaining high standards of learning and student development.

We provide a comprehensive educational journey from Pre-primary through Class 10. Our curriculum is delivered in Hindi medium, and we proudly prepare our senior students for the State Board examinations, ensuring they build a strong academic foundation for their future endeavors.
